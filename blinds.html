A Proper Sales Page, Not Just a Product List

Each block below is there to educate, qualify and move the customer closer to booking. That is the whole point of a strong service page.

Roller Blinds

The cleanest all-rounder. Roller blinds suit modern homes, apartments and investment properties because they are simple, sharp and easy to live with.

Minimal look Easy operation Blockout or sunscreen

Best for: living rooms, bedrooms, rentals and homeowners who want a sleek finish without visual clutter.

Double Roller Blinds

One of the smartest upsells. You get a sunscreen blind for daytime privacy and light filtering, plus a blockout blind for stronger privacy and darkness when needed.

Day + night control Premium upgrade More flexible use

Best for: front rooms, master bedrooms, street-facing windows and clients who want more control.

Venetian Blinds

A more classic option that gives strong control over light angle and privacy. They work well when customers want a more structured, slatted look instead of a fabric finish.

Adjustable slats Classic feel Strong privacy control

Best for: kitchens, offices and spaces where directional light control matters.

Motorised Options

This is where the brand starts to feel truly premium. Motorisation adds convenience, cleaner operation and a stronger high-end perception.

Premium positioning Convenient control Modern homes

Best for: high windows, larger homes, modern builds and customers already leaning toward premium finishes.

FAQs

Questions That Stop Customers Hesitating

These can be expanded later with your exact sales answers and location-specific wording.

What is the difference between blockout and sunscreen blinds?

Blockout blinds reduce light far more and are better for privacy and darker rooms. Sunscreen blinds filter light and help preserve outside views during the day.

Are double roller blinds worth it?

Usually yes for customers who want more control. They cost more, but they solve more problems and often feel like a premium upgrade.

Can I get blinds motorised?

Yes. That section is already built into the page so you can pitch convenience and premium finish without rebuilding the layout later.

Do I need to know exactly what I want before asking for a quote?

No. In fact, many customers should not decide alone. The site should push uncertain buyers toward advice instead of losing them.
