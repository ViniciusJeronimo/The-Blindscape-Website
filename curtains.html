Curtain types

Sell the Look, Then Back It Up With Function

Curtains are an emotional product. They are not just about covering glass. They shape mood, softness and perceived quality in the home.

Sheer Curtains

Sheers are the easiest way to make a room feel softer, brighter and more expensive. They filter daylight beautifully and create movement in the space.

Soft filtered light Elegant finish Premium feel

Best for: living rooms, master bedrooms, open-plan spaces and clients who care about the visual finish of the room.

Blockout Curtains

Blockout curtains bring stronger privacy, better darkness and a more substantial finish. They are practical, but still feel warm and premium when done properly.

More privacy Better sleep Fuller look

Best for: bedrooms, theatre rooms and any area where privacy and light reduction matter more.

Layered Curtains

Layering sheers with blockouts is one of the strongest upgrades you can sell. It gives the customer both softness and serious function.

Day + night use Luxury feel More flexibility

Best for: premium homes, renovation projects and clients who want the room to feel complete.

Tracks & Finishing Details

Details matter. Curtain track choice, fullness and drop all affect whether the finished product feels average or premium.

Clean finish Custom look Design impact

Best for: clients who notice detail and want the final room to feel intentionally designed.

FAQs

Questions That Help Close the Sale

These can be refined later with your real answers, lead times and installation details.

Are sheer curtains enough for privacy?

During the day, often yes. At night, privacy depends on lighting conditions, so some clients prefer layered curtains for better overall control.

What is the benefit of layered curtains?

You get the soft look of sheers during the day and the stronger privacy and darkness of blockouts when needed. It is one of the best premium upgrades.

Do curtains make a room feel warmer?

Visually, definitely. They soften hard lines, add texture and make spaces feel more complete and comfortable.

Can I use curtains with other window furnishings?

Yes. Curtains can work alongside blinds or shutters depending on the look and function the customer wants.
